ticklab

Source on GitHub

A timegrapher for mechanical watches. It listens to the ticking and measures how fast the watch runs, how even its beat is and how far the balance swings.

Measure

Hold the watch against the microphone in a quiet room. The audio is analysed on this page and is not stored.
